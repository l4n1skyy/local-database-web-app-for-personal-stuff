Connections

IBKR: an Activity Flex Query (Trades, Cash Transactions, Open Positions, Cash Report; XML, dates as yyyyMMdd) and a Flex Web Service token, both from Performance & Reports → Flex Queries. Wise: a read-only personal API token. Both tokens only read, and stay in finance.db on this computer.

Each time you open Finance (unless it already did in the last 5 minutes) the app fetches prices from Yahoo Finance, IBKR's statement and Wise's statements, and nothing in between. It adds new trades, deposits and transfers, corrects any the app has that IBKR or Wise says differently, and adds the day's prices. It never deletes anything, and doesn't keep the statements themselves, only the trades, deposits, transfers and prices from them.
